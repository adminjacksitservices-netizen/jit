Legal Obligation

Legitimate Interests

9. Data Retention

We do not keep personal information indefinitely. We retain information only for as long as it is reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law.

Two-Year Customer Inactivity Period

If you have not made or completed another appointment with Jacks IT Services for a period of two years, we will normally review your customer record.

Following this review, personal information that we no longer have a legitimate, necessary or legal reason to retain will normally be securely deleted or anonymised.

This may include customer contact details, addresses, appointment information and other personal information associated with an inactive customer record.

Important: The two-year deletion period does not mean that every record will automatically be deleted after two years.

Certain information, including invoices, accounting records, tax records and other business records, may need to be retained for longer where required by UK law.

For example, business records may need to be retained to satisfy HMRC requirements. Where a legal or regulatory obligation requires information to be retained, we will retain it for the required period and will not use it for unrelated purposes where unnecessary.

We may also retain limited information for longer where reasonably necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, comply with legal obligations or protect our legitimate interests.

When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to securely delete or anonymise it.
